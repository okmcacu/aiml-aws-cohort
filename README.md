# AI/ML AWS Cohort

My work for the **AWS Student Builder Group (AASTU) – AI/ML Track, Cohort 1** (Oct – Dec 2026).

---

## Index

| Week | Lab | What it does | Tech |
|------|-----|--------------|------|
| 1 | [Personal Expense Analyzer](ML-WEEK1/personal_expense_analyzer) | Cleans a messy CSV of personal expenses (in ETB) and summarises spending by category and month. | Python, pandas, NumPy |
| 2 | [Medical FAQ Semantic Search](ML-WEEK2/semantic-search-lab) | Cleans a medical FAQ CSV, chunks the answers, and returns the closest chunks for a question. | Python, pandas, NumPy, Chroma, `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` embeddings |
| 3 | [Break the Spam Filter](ML-WEEK3/spam-filter-lab) | Trains a TF-IDF + Naive Bayes spam detector, then tries to fool it and explains why it fails. | Python, scikit-learn, pandas |
